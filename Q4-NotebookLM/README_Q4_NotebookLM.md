# Q4 -- NotebookLM Evaluation: ESG and Sustainability Learning

## Overview

This folder documents Question 4 of BUS5001 Assessment 3. The experiment
evaluates NotebookLM as an academic assistant for exploring
sustainability reporting and the United Nations Sustainable Development
Goals (SDGs).

**Notebook link:** [Open the Q4 NotebookLM
notebook](https://notebook.google.com/notebook/27675f74-eec7-46d2-b773-35240b9b9410)

The notebook link is included for the assessor. Please check that the
sharing settings allow the teaching team to open it; access may
otherwise depend on the Google account used.

## Sources used

Two sources were added to the notebook:

1.  **GRI -- Standards** --- information about the Global Reporting
    Initiative's sustainability reporting standards.
2.  **THE 17 GOALS \| Sustainable Development** --- the United Nations'
    overview of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals.

The experiment used these sources to explore source-grounded summaries,
quiz generation, mind mapping, and an Audio Overview.

## Activities completed

-   Generated a summary of key ideas from the selected sources and
    inspected the inline source citations.
-   Generated a 10-question multiple-choice quiz and reviewed the
    feedback provided for answers.
-   Generated a mind map titled **Sustainability Mind Map**, with the
    central topic **Sustainability Reporting & Global Goals** and
    branches for GRI and the UN SDGs.
-   Generated an Audio Overview titled **How Companies Track
    Sustainability Impact**, approximately 1 minute and 27 seconds long.

More detailed steps, observations, and limitations are recorded in
[`experiment-log.md`](experiment-log.md).

## Main observations

NotebookLM helped organise the selected materials into different
learning formats. The source-grounded summary displayed citations that
could be checked against the supplied sources. The quiz provided
feedback on answers; the recorded result was **9/10 (90%)**. The mind
map offered a high-level view of the two source themes, while the Audio
Overview presented the material in an audio discussion format.

These observations describe the outputs produced in this experiment.
They do not establish that every generated statement was correct or that
the tool can replace reading and evaluating original sources.

## Limitations and responsible use

-   The results depend on the two sources added to the notebook; they do
    not represent a comprehensive review of ESG research.
-   A citation should be opened and checked to confirm that it supports
    the related claim.
-   Generated quizzes can contain incorrect or ambiguous questions or
    answer keys, so feedback should be verified against the original
    source.
-   Mind maps simplify relationships and may omit nuance or detailed
    mappings between GRI standards and individual SDG targets.
-   The Audio Overview was generated but was not independently verified
    word-for-word against a transcript.
-   Users should avoid uploading confidential, personal, or restricted
    information unless authorised to do so.

NotebookLM's own documentation describes features such as
source-grounded answers, mind maps, and Audio Overviews; generated
material should still be critically evaluated. See the official
[NotebookLM Help
Centre](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16246230?hl=en) and
Google's overview of [Audio
Overviews](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/notebooklm-audio-overviews/).
