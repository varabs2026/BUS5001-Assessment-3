The findings below are based on the notebook outputs observed during the

## 3. Experiment 1 --- Source-grounded summary

**Action:** Asked NotebookLM to summarise key points from the selected
sources.

**Observed output:** The summary highlighted the UN 2030 Agenda and 17
SDGs, connections between social, economic, and environmental
dimensions, ESG impacts, standardised sustainability reporting, and
transparency. It described the SDGs as a global sustainability framework
and GRI as a reporting-standards resource. Numbered citations were
displayed with the response.

**Evaluation:** Inline citations make it easier to trace claims to the
selected material. A citation is not proof by itself: each source
passage must be opened to confirm that it supports the claim. Because
only two sources were provided, the summary does not cover all corporate
ESG metrics, financial ESG models, or the full research literature.

## 4. Experiment 2 --- Multiple-choice quiz

**Action:** Generated a 10-question multiple-choice quiz and reviewed
the answers and feedback.

**Observed examples:** - For the question about the GRI Standards
structure, the answer **"Universal Standards, Sector Standards, and
Topic Standards"** was marked correct. - For a question about the date,
the selected answer **"1 October 2021"** was marked incorrect.
NotebookLM's feedback identified **"1 January 2023"** as the correct
answer. - The displayed final result was **9/10 (90%)**.

**Evaluation:** The quiz supports active recall and provides immediate
feedback. However, generated questions and answer keys can be wrong,
unclear, or dependent on context. The date question demonstrates the
importance of checking feedback against the original GRI source rather
than accepting it without verification. The score is the score displayed
by NotebookLM for this quiz; it is not a measure of the tool's overall
factual accuracy.

## 5. Experiment 3 --- Mind map

**Action:** Generated a mind map titled **"Sustainability Mind Map."**

**Observed output:** The central topic was **"Sustainability Reporting &
Global Goals."** Main branches included GRI and UN SDGs.

**Evaluation:** The visual structure helps organise the two source
themes and can support initial exploration. It remains high-level and
should not be treated as a complete mapping of GRI standards to
individual SDGs or SDG targets. Detailed relationships should be checked
against the original standards and UN material.

## 6. Experiment 4 --- Audio Overview

**Action:** Generated an Audio Overview titled **"How Companies Track
Sustainability Impact."**

**Observed output:** The generated audio was approximately **1 minute
and 27 seconds** long and was shown playing in NotebookLM.

**Evaluation:** An audio discussion offers an alternative way to review
source material and may help with initial understanding. The audio was
not independently checked word-for-word against a transcript during this
experiment. It should therefore be treated as a learning aid, not as a
definitive or exhaustive account of the sources.

Google describes Audio Overviews as AI-generated discussions based on
supplied sources and notes that they may contain inaccuracies. See:
https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/notebooklm-audio-overviews/

## 7. Overall assessment

The experiment showed that NotebookLM can transform a small source
collection into several useful study formats: a cited summary, a quiz
with feedback, a mind map, and an audio discussion. These outputs can
make source material easier to explore, but the quality of the result
remains dependent on the selected sources and the accuracy of the
generated content.

For academic use, the recommended workflow is to: 1. Select credible and
relevant primary sources. 2. Use NotebookLM to identify themes and
generate study aids. 3. Open and verify citations against the original
source. 4. Check quiz answers and dates independently. 5. Use generated
mind maps and audio as summaries, not substitutes for reading. 6.
Disclose AI assistance where required by the course's academic-integrity
policy.
